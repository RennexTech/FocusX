You must use nuitka not pyinstaller, coz pyinstaller is kinda high-level and doesn't hook keyboard and mouse to prevent usage during a break.

Nuitka allows us to lock the keyboard and mouse during pomodoro breaks, so that you MUST go chill! 

And then, the app requirements must be installed before compiling with nuitka.

You can also check out the previous versions to see which one might impress you, compile with nuitka and see if any works for you.

Version 15 is my favorite, coz i just place it inside shell:startup and it launches and autostarts on every boot.
